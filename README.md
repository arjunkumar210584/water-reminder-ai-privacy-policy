# Privacy Policy

**Water Reminder AI**
Package name: `water.reminder.ai`
Last updated: 12 October 2026

Water Reminder AI is an offline-first hydration app. There is no account and no login. This policy explains what stays on your device and what is sent only if you turn an optional feature on.

Contact: arjunkumar210584@gmail.com

## Data stored on your device

The app stores your data locally on the phone. This includes:

- Drink logs, daily targets, streaks, cups, and reminder settings
- An optional display name and an optional profile photo
- Optional profile details used only to suggest a water target (weight, age range, sex, activity, climate)
- AI consent status and cached coach text
- An optional encrypted or plain backup that you export yourself

Android auto-backup of this app data is turned off. You can export a backup, import a backup, or erase everything in Settings by typing RESET. Erasing data deletes it from the device.

Your name and photo are not put in reminder text.

## Data we do not require

- No email, phone number, or password
- No contacts, microphone, or location
- No advertising ID
- No sale of your data

## Optional cloud coach

Cloud coaching is off until you turn it on and accept a preview of what will be sent. You can revoke that consent and delete cached AI data in Settings.

If you consent, the app sends aggregate numbers only to Google Gemini through Firebase AI Logic. Those numbers can include intake totals, streak, typical drinking hours, missed reminders, drink-type counts, and flags for workout, travel, and your climate setting.

The cloud coach does not receive your name, photo, weight, age, sex, or exact location, and it does not receive your Health Connect records.

A photo used for optional drink logging stays on the device unless you confirm a cloud estimate. That confirmation can send the photo so the model can suggest a container size and drink type. Nothing is saved until you confirm the result.

Firebase App Check is used to reduce abuse of the AI quota. A silent Firebase installation token may be created for that service. It is not a user account, and the app never asks you to sign in.

## Health Connect

Health Connect is optional and off by default. If you turn writing on, the app can write today’s hydrating total. If you turn reading on, the app can read workouts to adjust a suggested target on the device. Tracking works if Health Connect is not available.

## Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules hydration reminders on the device. Reminder text does not include your name or photo.

## Crash reports and analytics

If Firebase is available, the app may send crash reports through Firebase Crashlytics and privacy-safe event names through Firebase Analytics, such as whether a cloud coach call succeeded. These reports do not include your drink log, name, or photo. The advertising ID is not collected.

## Subscriptions

Hydration AI Plus is optional. Logging, reminders, history, insights, cups, streaks, and the water target stay available without a subscription.

The plans shown in the app are ₹99 monthly, ₹399 for 6 months, and ₹699 annually. This version does not start a purchase. When billing is enabled, Google Play processes the payment. The app does not receive your card number.

## Children

Water Reminder AI is a general wellness app and is not directed at children under 13.
